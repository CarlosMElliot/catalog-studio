# Reference-inspired catalog layouts

The original eight designs remain available with unchanged identifiers and survey indices. New designs are appended, and barcode settings apply to every design. The interface and simple import → images → layout → export workflow are unchanged.

## Wholesale, distribution and grocery

- Weekly Featured: landscape six-card flyer, red prices, pale cards and navy ordering footer.
- Weekend Deals: twelve-card portrait flyer, navy/gold masthead and blue price labels.
- Wholesale Range: purple category panels, up to eight products per page; table of contents uses actual grouped page positions.

Optional campaign details live in Brand & cover. Previous price, price unit and offer label live in the product editor. These promotion fields are manual fields, preserved in saved catalogs and backups; spreadsheet mapping does not yet expose them. A previous price prints only when it is higher than the current price. No sale, certification or discount is invented.

## Fashion, clothing and accessories

Seasonal Fashion (4), Bold Fashion (2), Warm Lookbook (2), Minimal Collection (4), Bridal Elegance (1), and Menswear Edit (9) provide automatic layouts without page editing. Numbers indicate products per page. Two-panel designs use large images beside their descriptions.

## Beauty, wellness and personal care

Aqua Skincare (4) uses aqua/white panels, Beauty Editorial (2) pairs large photos with product stories, and Care Collection (3) uses wide cream/rose panels. These draw on the four supplied skincare and beauty examples. The three cosmetic promotion images in the fashion batch also inform this category.

References guide spacing, hierarchy and palette; their watermarked photos, logos, claims, testimonials and prices are not copied. Catalogs use the customer's own product image, description and branding. One image per product is currently supported. Product pages use the reference palette, while typography remains selectable. Covers retain the standard branded cover structure.

All layouts support optional vector barcodes and full-resolution source images in the existing 300-DPI PDF pipeline. Short descriptions may be needed in dense designs; export checks for overflowing product text. Browser rendering and physical scanner checks have not been performed in this environment.
